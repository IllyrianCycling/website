A DAY ON TOUR

06:30

Breakfast & Briefing

Performance breakfast with macro-balanced fueling. Route briefing covers elevation profile, weather, pacing plan, and feed zones.

07:30

Roll Out

Groups depart by ability level. Support vehicle follows with spare wheels, nutrition, and mechanical backup. Lead and sweep guides manage pace.

10:00

Summit Feed Zone

Planned nutrition stop at the climb summit. Gels, bars, electrolytes, and coffee. Regroup and prepare for the descent.

13:00

Return & Recover

Post-ride recovery protocol: protein shake, foam rolling, optional physio. Secure bike wash and overnight maintenance check.

19:00

Dinner & Route Preview

Evening meal with the group. Tomorrow's route overview with elevation analysis and pacing strategy. Sleep is part of the programme.
