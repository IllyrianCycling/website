Illyrian-Led Performance

THE FULL EXPERIENCE
From our monthly fixed-date Performance Camps to bespoke 3, 5 or 7-day Performance Blocks, Illyrian delivers coached riding experiences built around Montenegro's terrain.

Every programme brings together purposeful route selection, performance-led pacing, on-ride support and structured recovery. Illyrian leads the riding and manages execution, with the format shaped by the programme and the demands of the terrain.

Groups are limited to eight riders to maintain coaching attention and controlled delivery.

Pacing Groups

Riders grouped by FTP and/or climbing ability, with pacing adapted to the terrain, daily objectives and accumulated training load.

On-Ride Support

Support vehicle, spares, tools, mechanic and on-bike nutrition to support the riding programme.

Recovery

Recovery protocols integrated into the programme, balancing daily demands with accumulated load.

Transfers & Stays

Hand-selected accommodation with secure bike storage, with transfers coordinated around the programme.
