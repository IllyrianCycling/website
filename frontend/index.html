Mountain Passes

Dinaric Alps including Lovćen and Sedlo deliver sustained gradients and alpine exposure. Roads built for climbers, not commuters.

Interior Roads

Secondary routes and mountain passes. The kind of roads where your rhythm is only broken by your own breath.

Compact Variety

In a single week: Coastal sprints, 1,500m ascents, medieval stone hamlets, and technical descents. Peak riding - seamless logistics.
