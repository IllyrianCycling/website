1. Bookings & Payment

  • A booking is confirmed once the required booking deposit has been received and confirmation has been issued by Illyrian Cycling.
  • Deposits are paid through our secure Stripe payment page and are credited towards the total programme price.
  • The remaining balance is payable by bank transfer by the deadline specified in your booking confirmation.
  • Stripe payments cover Illyrian Cycling's guided and coached cycling service only. They do not include accommodation, transport or other third-party services.
  • All prices, payment amounts and applicable deadlines are communicated before booking.
  • Each camp has a maximum capacity of six riders.
  • Failure to pay the outstanding balance by the agreed deadline may result in cancellation of the booking following written notification.

Our agreement with participants covers the guided and coached cycling programme described in the relevant camp listing.

2. Cancellations & Refunds

By the participant:

Cancellations must be submitted in writing by email. The date on which the written cancellation email is received by Illyrian Cycling is the date used for all time calculations below. The 60-day period is calculated in calendar days from that date.

  • More than 60 days before camp: All payments will be refunded, less the booking deposit. Retention of the booking deposit is subject to validation under applicable statutory consumer law.
  • 60 days or fewer before camp: No refund.
  • Booking transfer: Participants may request to transfer their booking to another eligible person, subject to prior written approval by Illyrian Cycling. Transfers are permitted only where that approval has been given in writing before the camp start date.

The booking deposit is a part payment towards the total programme price and is disclosed before any payment is made.

Any statutory consumer rights applicable to your booking remain unaffected. Where a statutory exception applies to leisure activities scheduled for specific dates, the relevant withdrawal rights may not apply.

By Illyrian Cycling:

We reserve the right to cancel a camp or programme block where necessary due to force majeure, insufficient participant numbers, adverse weather, safety concerns or other significant operational circumstances.

If Illyrian Cycling cancels a camp or programme block, participants may choose between:

  • a full refund of all payments made to Illyrian Cycling for the affected programme, including the booking deposit, with zero deductions and no subtraction of Stripe processing fees; or
  • a transfer to an alternative camp date, subject to availability.

Refunds under this section are made to the original payment method. Independently incurred expenses, such as flights, independently booked accommodation or independently arranged transport, remain separate from payments made to Illyrian Cycling and are the participant's own responsibility, subject to any liability imposed by applicable law.
